# Business Insights & Recommendations

## Executive Summary

The cleaned portfolio dataset contains 1,200 e-commerce transactions. The analysis focuses on revenue, profit, orders, units sold, product performance, regions, categories and sales channels.

| Units Sold | 5,364 |
| Average Order Value | ₹10,486 |

## Key Findings

### 1. Regional Performance
**West** generated the highest revenue among the analyzed regions.

**Recommendation:** Investigate the products, customer mix and channel performance contributing to the West region's stronger results and assess whether successful practices can be replicated in other regions.

### 2. Category Performance
**Electronics** was the highest-revenue category.

**Recommendation:** Monitor both revenue and profit margin for Electronics so growth is evaluated on profitability as well as sales volume.

### 3. Product Performance
**Storage Set** ranked first by revenue among the products analyzed.

**Recommendation:** Review demand patterns, pricing and margin for high-revenue products and use the findings to support inventory and promotional decisions.

### 4. Sales Channel Performance
The **Website** generated the highest revenue among the sales channels.

**Recommendation:** Prioritize analysis of website conversion and repeat-purchase behavior while identifying opportunities to improve weaker channels.

## Recommended Business Actions

1. **Protect profitable growth:** Track revenue and profit together rather than optimizing revenue alone.
2. **Replicate regional success:** Compare product mix, channel mix and customer behavior in West against lower-performing regions.
3. **Review product economics:** Evaluate discounting and margins for high-revenue products.
4. **Improve channel performance:** Use channel-level KPIs to identify conversion and retention opportunities.
5. **Monitor trends:** Review monthly revenue and profit movement to detect changes that require investigation.

> **Data note:** All figures and findings are calculated from the portfolio dataset in this repository. The dataset is created for portfolio and learning purposes and is not presented as real company data.
